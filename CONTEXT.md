# Crafting and material trading

Terms used to distinguish crafting needs from trade-cart activity.

## Language

**Trading post**: A location with a defined set of packs that can be created there and rewards available for eligible deliveries.

**Trade pack**: A bundle made from materials at its origin post and exchanged for a specified reward at an eligible destination.

**Trade run**: Loading up to seven mixed packs at one origin post and delivering them to one or more destinations. Loading another cart starts another run.
_Avoid_: Charge, delivery charge

**Delivery stop**: A destination where loaded packs are exchanged. A run can supply multiple delivery stops without counting as more runs.

**Base reward**: The expected material payout before any market-price extras.

**Extra received**: Material units actually received above a delivery's base reward. This is a bonus count, not the total receipt or a market-price percentage.

**Protected runs**: A run and all preceding runs whose instructions remain fixed during bonus recalculation. Clearing extras does not return these runs to future planning.

**Future runs**: Later, uncommitted runs that can change when recorded extras reduce or increase remaining material needs.

**Channel budget**: Three daily runs for PvE and a separate three for conflict channels. PvE-only players use only the first budget.

**Spare stock**: On-hand materials remaining after reserving what the chosen craft needs. Only spare stock can fund packs.

**Inventory snapshot**: The player's complete on-hand stock at one unchanged point in time. Bag and Storage quantities count separately, while repeated views of the same stack count only once.

**Farming selection**: Materials the player is willing to acquire in addition to on-hand stock for making trade packs.

**Trade days**: Days required by daily run limits with full daily channel budgets. This excludes farming and travel time and does not imply all crafting shortages are covered.

**Crafting plan**: One item and quantity to make, with its own inventory, unlock assumptions, acquisition choices and execution progress. Different plans are independent.
_Avoid_: Saved trade plan as the primary goal

**Plan inventory**: Stock available to one crafting plan. It can include gathered resources, crafted ingredients, trade materials and the target item.

**Equivalent recipes**: Recipes with the same material quantities per output item, differing only in batch size.

**Master recipe**: An alternate processing recipe using a rare ingredient instead of ordinary materials. It is eligible only with the relevant profession's Master unlock and available spare rare stock.

**Outdated runs**: Previously configured trade instructions whose planning inputs have changed. Their instructions, progress and recorded extras remain available, but they no longer reduce the current plan's crafting needs until reviewed and rebuilt.

**Completed step**: A gathering task, trade run or crafting action marked finished by the player. Completion records progress only, without changing inventory or the remaining instruction quantities.
